Bespoke perforating, fabrication and finishing

Because we perforate in-house, we're not limited to the catalogue. Specify your own hole size and pitch, blank (unperforated) margins and areas, architectural panels, or have stock sheet cut, folded, rolled, edged and coated ready to fit.

  • Specific hole sizes, pitches and patterns to your drawing
  • Unperforated margins and areas for fixing and welding
  • Cutting, bending, rolling, edging and fixing holes
  • Powder coating (full RAL range), anodising, polishing and electropolishing
  • Raised perforated walkway treadplate in steel, aluminium and stainless
