About C4 U7 aluminium perforated sheet

C4 U7 is a square hole pattern with 4mm squares on a 7mm square pitch, leaving 3mm bars between holes, giving 32% open area. That is a moderate open area that balances airflow and visibility against strength, the usual territory for guards and general-purpose screens. Patterns of this type are typically used for guards, screens and ceilings needing maximum open area.

Aluminium is about a third of the weight of steel, does not rust and takes anodising and powder coating well, which makes it the architectural favourite. This pattern is held in 1.5mm thickness in 2000 x 1000mm sheets, made to order on a 5–7 day lead time. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.

Need a different hole size, pitch or thickness? We perforate to order. Use the open area and weight calculator to check alternatives, or send us your specification.
