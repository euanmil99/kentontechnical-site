About R20 U48.5 stainless steel perforated sheet

R20 U48.5 is a round hole, square pitch pattern with 20mm rounds on a 48.5mm square pitch, leaving 28.5mm bars between holes, giving 13% open area. That is a relatively low open area, giving a strong, rigid sheet with a fine, almost solid appearance from a distance. Patterns of this type are typically used for architectural screens, shop fittings and panels where the grid pattern is on show.

Stainless steel gives hygiene, corrosion resistance and a bright self-finish for food, marine, chemical and architectural work; grade 316 is available to order. This pattern is held in 1.5mm thickness in 2000 x 1000mm sheets, made to order on a 5–7 day lead time. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.

Need a different hole size, pitch or thickness? We perforate to order. Use the open area and weight calculator to check alternatives, or send us your specification.
