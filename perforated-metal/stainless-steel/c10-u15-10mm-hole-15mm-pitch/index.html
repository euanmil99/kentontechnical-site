About C10 U15 stainless steel perforated sheet

C10 U15 is a square hole pattern with 10mm squares on a 15mm square pitch, leaving 5mm bars between holes, giving 44% open area. That is a high open area that suits ventilation, acoustic facings and screens where see-through matters, while keeping useful stiffness. Patterns of this type are typically used for guards, screens and ceilings needing maximum open area.

Stainless steel gives hygiene, corrosion resistance and a bright self-finish for food, marine, chemical and architectural work; grade 316 is available to order. This pattern is held in 1.0mm, 1.5mm, 2.0mm thicknesses in 3000 x 1500mm and 2500 x 1250mm and 2000 x 1000mm sheets; items marked in stock ship next working day. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.

Need a different hole size, pitch or thickness? We perforate to order. Use the open area and weight calculator to check alternatives, or send us your specification.
