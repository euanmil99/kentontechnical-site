About R10 T13 stainless steel perforated sheet

R10 T13 is a round hole, staggered pitch pattern with 10mm rounds on a 13mm staggered pitch, leaving 3mm bars between holes, giving 54% open area. That is a high open area that suits ventilation, acoustic facings and screens where see-through matters, while keeping useful stiffness. Patterns of this type are typically used for general screening, guarding, ventilation and acoustic facings.

Stainless steel gives hygiene, corrosion resistance and a bright self-finish for food, marine, chemical and architectural work; grade 316 is available to order. This pattern is held in 1.0mm thickness in 2000 x 1000mm sheets, made to order on a 5–7 day lead time. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.

Need a different hole size, pitch or thickness? We perforate to order. Use the open area and weight calculator to check alternatives, or send us your specification.
