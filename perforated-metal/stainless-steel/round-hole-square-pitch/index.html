Perforated stainless steel

Round hole, square pitch perforated stainless steel sheet

Round holes in straight rows and columns. Lower open area than staggered, but a neat rectilinear look that aligns with panel edges and fixings.

Round staggeredRound square pitchSquare holeSlotted

We hold 3 round hole square pitch patterns in stainless steel from stock, with hole sizes from 4.5mm to 20mm and open areas from 7% to 13%, in thicknesses of 1.5mm to 1.5mm. Every pattern below has its own page with full specifications, sheet sizes and lead times; anything not listed can be perforated to order.

Round hole square pitch (DIN 24041 "Rg", pattern code R hole U pitch) sets the holes in straight rows and columns at 90° to each other. For the same hole size and pitch it gives about 13% less open area than the staggered layout, but the pattern reads as a clean grid, lines up with panel edges and fixing holes, and is easier to align across adjacent panels. It is often chosen for architectural screens, shop fittings and decorative panels where the pattern itself is on show.

Because the bars between holes run only in two directions, square-pitch sheet is slightly less stiff than staggered sheet of the same specification, which is rarely an issue at the open areas involved.

Grade 304 from stock, 316 to order. Hygienic, corrosion resistant and attractive, for food, marine, architectural and chemical environments.
