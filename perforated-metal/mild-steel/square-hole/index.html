Perforated mild steel

Square hole perforated mild steel sheet

Square holes on a straight pitch. Very high open areas, up to 64%, with a crisp modern appearance for screens and guards.

Round staggeredRound square pitchSquare holeSlotted

We hold 10 square hole patterns in mild steel from stock, with hole sizes from 5mm to 25mm and open areas from 39% to 69%, in thicknesses of 1mm to 3mm. Every pattern below has its own page with full specifications, sheet sizes and lead times; anything not listed can be perforated to order.

Square hole perforated sheet (DIN 24041 "Qg", pattern code C hole U pitch) punches square openings in straight rows. A square hole has 27% more area than a round hole of the same width, so this pattern achieves the highest open areas in the catalogue, up to 64% in our C8 U10, with correspondingly low resistance to air and light. The straight bars give a strong, contemporary grid appearance that suits guards, screens, ceilings and facade panels.

Square patterns are slightly less stiff than round staggered patterns of similar open area, and the sharp internal corners concentrate stress, so for heavy-duty guarding we usually recommend 2mm or thicker material.

The economical general-purpose choice: strong, easy to weld, fold and powder coat. Supplied uncoated (self-colour) for painting or coating after fabrication.
