6mm perforated mild steel sheet

Every mild steel perforation available in 6mm, with sizes, open area and weight.

1mm1.5mm2mm3mm4mm5mm6mm

6mm is a thickness for perforated mild steel: we hold 3 patterns in 6mm, 2 of them on the shelf for next-working-day delivery and the rest on a 5–7 day lead time. The table lists every pattern with its open area, the sheet sizes available in this thickness and the approximate weight per square metre.

Heavier sheet for walkways, large unsupported panels, heavy screens and anywhere impact resistance matters. Hole sizes are limited to roughly the sheet thickness and above. Use the open area and weight calculator to compare thicknesses, or see all perforated mild steel patterns.
