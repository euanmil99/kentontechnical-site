About R12 T16 mild steel perforated sheet

R12 T16 is a round hole, staggered pitch pattern with 12mm rounds on a 16mm staggered pitch, leaving 4mm bars between holes, giving 51% open area. That is a high open area that suits ventilation, acoustic facings and screens where see-through matters, while keeping useful stiffness. Patterns of this type are typically used for general screening, guarding, ventilation and acoustic facings.

Mild steel is the economical choice and takes powder coating well after fabrication; it is supplied self-colour and needs a finish for exterior use. This pattern is held in 1.5mm, 2.0mm, 3.0mm, 4.0mm, 10.0mm thicknesses in 2500 x 1250mm and 2000 x 1000mm sheets; items marked in stock ship next working day. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.
