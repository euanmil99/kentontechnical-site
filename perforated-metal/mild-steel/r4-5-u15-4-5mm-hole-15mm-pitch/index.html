About R4.5 U15 mild steel perforated sheet

R4.5 U15 is a round hole, square pitch pattern with 4.5mm rounds on a 15mm square pitch, leaving 10.5mm bars between holes, giving 7% open area. That is a relatively low open area, giving a strong, rigid sheet with a fine, almost solid appearance from a distance. Patterns of this type are typically used for architectural screens, shop fittings and panels where the grid pattern is on show.

Mild steel is the economical choice and takes powder coating well after fabrication; it is supplied self-colour and needs a finish for exterior use. This pattern is held in 1.0mm, 1.5mm, 2.0mm thicknesses in 2500 x 1250mm and 2000 x 1000mm sheets; items marked in stock ship next working day. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.

Need a different hole size, pitch or thickness? We perforate to order. Use the open area and weight calculator to check alternatives, or send us your specification.
