About C20 U25 mild steel perforated sheet

C20 U25 is a square hole pattern with 20mm squares on a 25mm square pitch, leaving 5mm bars between holes, giving 62% open area. That is a very high open area, so it offers minimal resistance to air, light and liquid and is among the lightest patterns for its thickness, at the cost of stiffness. Patterns of this type are typically used for guards, screens and ceilings needing maximum open area.

Mild steel is the economical choice and takes powder coating well after fabrication; it is supplied self-colour and needs a finish for exterior use. This pattern is held in 2.0mm thickness in 2500 x 1250mm sheets, made to order on a 5–7 day lead time. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.

Need a different hole size, pitch or thickness? We perforate to order. Use the open area and weight calculator to check alternatives, or send us your specification.
