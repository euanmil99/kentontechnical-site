Every pre-galvanised steel perforation available in 0.75mm, with sizes, open area and weight.

0.75mm is a thickness for perforated pre-galvanised steel: we hold 3 patterns in 0.75mm, 1 of them on the shelf for next-working-day delivery and the rest on a 5–7 day lead time. The table lists every pattern with its open area, the sheet sizes available in this thickness and the approximate weight per square metre.

Thin sheet suits fine patterns, decorative panels, grilles and covers, and keeps weight down; stiffen larger panels with a folded return. Use the open area and weight calculator to compare thicknesses, or see all perforated pre-galvanised steel patterns.
