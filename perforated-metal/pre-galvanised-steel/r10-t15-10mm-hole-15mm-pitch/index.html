About R10 T15 pre-galvanised steel perforated sheet

R10 T15 is a round hole, staggered pitch pattern with 10mm rounds on a 15mm staggered pitch, leaving 5mm bars between holes, giving 40% open area. That is a high open area that suits ventilation, acoustic facings and screens where see-through matters, while keeping useful stiffness. Patterns of this type are typically used for general screening, guarding, ventilation and acoustic facings.

Pre-galvanised steel gives good corrosion resistance for exterior and agricultural use without the cost of stainless. This pattern is held in 1.0mm, 1.5mm, 2.0mm, 3.0mm thicknesses in 3000 x 1500mm and 2500 x 1250mm and 2000 x 1000mm sheets; items marked in stock ship next working day. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.
