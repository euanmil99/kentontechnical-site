About R5 T7 pre-galvanised steel perforated sheet

R5 T7 is a round hole, staggered pitch pattern with 5mm rounds on a 7mm staggered pitch, leaving 2mm bars between holes, giving 46% open area. That is a high open area that suits ventilation, acoustic facings and screens where see-through matters, while keeping useful stiffness. Patterns of this type are typically used for general screening, guarding, ventilation and acoustic facings.

Pre-galvanised steel gives good corrosion resistance for exterior and agricultural use without the cost of stainless. This pattern is held in 1.0mm thickness in 3000 x 1500mm and 2500 x 1250mm and 2000 x 1000mm sheets; items marked in stock ship next working day. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.

Need a different hole size, pitch or thickness? We perforate to order. Use the open area and weight calculator to check alternatives, or send us your specification.
