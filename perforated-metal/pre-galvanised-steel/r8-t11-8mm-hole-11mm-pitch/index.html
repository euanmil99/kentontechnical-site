About R8 T11 pre-galvanised steel perforated sheet

R8 T11 is a round hole, staggered pitch pattern with 8mm rounds on a 11mm staggered pitch, leaving 3mm bars between holes, giving 48% open area. That is a high open area that suits ventilation, acoustic facings and screens where see-through matters, while keeping useful stiffness. Patterns of this type are typically used for general screening, guarding, ventilation and acoustic facings.

Pre-galvanised steel gives good corrosion resistance for exterior and agricultural use without the cost of stainless. This pattern is held in 1.0mm, 1.5mm thicknesses in 2000 x 1000mm sheets, made to order on a 5–7 day lead time. Stock sheet has small side margins and the pattern runs off the sheet ends; we can cut to size, leave unperforated margins or areas to your drawing, fold, roll and coat: see fabrication and coating.
