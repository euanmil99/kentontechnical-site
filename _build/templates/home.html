About Kenton Technical Products

Supplying UK industry from Bristol since 1986

Centred in Bristol, Kenton Technical Products Ltd supplies perforated and expanded metals, woven and welded wire to customers across the UK. Established in 1986, our success has been built on manufacturing and supplying top-quality products at competitive prices with excellent customer service.
