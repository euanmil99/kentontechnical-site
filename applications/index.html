Applications

Perforated metal applications

Which pattern, material and thickness for the job, with the stock perforations we recommend most often.

By application

Perforated metal for machine guarding and enclosures

Guards that keep hands out and let operators see in. Hole size by safety distance, material by environment.

Perforated metal for acoustic panels and sound absorption

Perforated facings that let sound reach the absorber behind. Open area, hole size and thickness for ceilings, walls and enclosures.

Perforated metal facades, cladding and architectural screens

Rainscreens, sunscreens, balustrade infill and feature panels in aluminium, stainless, galvanised and copper.

Perforated metal for screening, sieving, grading and drainage

Sizing screens, sieve trays, drying trays, drainage covers and filters: choosing hole size, shape and material for the product.

Perforated metal ventilation grilles, radiator covers and speaker grilles

Airflow with a finished appearance: patterns and materials for grilles, louvres, plinths, enclosure vents and cabinet panels.

Perforated metal balustrade infill, fencing and security screens

Infill panels for stairs, balconies and mezzanines, plus security screens and fencing where you need to see through but not climb or reach through.
